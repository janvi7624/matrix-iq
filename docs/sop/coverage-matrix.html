02Scope summary of each document

2.1 Accounts Module SOP

Covers. The Payments module end to end — the Payment Queue, Payment History and Reimbursement Report tabs; reviewing a payment and opening its original request; marking as paid with the per-source preconditions; placing and lifting holds; filtering and CSV export; how the target date is computed and how the queue is ordered. Plus the Audit Log, including its restriction to Super Admin and Admin.

Also covers, as context. The Accounts stage of five upstream chains — reimbursement, travel, bill of materials, admin expenses and office operation expenses — and a comparison of Billing against the payment queue.

Excludes. Bank operations, statutory filing, tax computation, and the creation of the records that become payments.

2.2 Administration Module SOP

Covers. All eleven configuration submodules: User Management including bulk import and employee exit, Role Management, Department Master, Module Manager, Custom Module Builder, Products, Product Catalog, Application Settings, Meta Lead Integration, Daily Report Configuration and the Audit Log. For each: what it controls elsewhere in the platform, how to change it safely, and what cannot be undone.

Distinctive content. The four inputs that decide visibility; the modules that deliberately refuse the privileged flag; a change-risk summary; and a recommended change-control process, since MatrixIQ has no approval workflow for configuration and the Audit Log does not record configuration edits.

Excludes. Deployment, database administration, environment variables, and the TMS Tab Access matrix.

2.3 HR Module SOP

Covers. All seven HR submodules: Employees, Office Operation Expenses, Reimbursement, Admin Expenses, HR Expense Report, Travel Schedule and Daily Report Review. The reimbursement and travel chains are documented end to end, from both the employee's and the approver's side.

Distinctive content. The two different access patterns — the HR role gate and the named-individual gate — and the deliberate split between reading and acting on daily reports.

Excludes. Payroll, statutory compliance, recruitment, appraisal mechanics, and payment settlement.

2.4 Marketing Module SOP

Covers. Marketing Requests from the four-step wizard through approval, timeline commitment, assignment, acceptance, production, technical review and delivery, with cancellation and decline treated separately. Plus the Materials library: three document sources, categories, the folder tree, and the two-gate partner publication rule.

Distinctive content. The full fourteen-status vocabulary with the mapping onto the five statuses a partner sees; and an ordering constraint — approve, then set the timeline, then assign — that is easy to get wrong.

Excludes. Brand guidelines, creative standards and agency management.

2.5 Operations Module SOP

Covers. The Delivery Challan lifecycle including the return verification checklist and the monthly summary; and the support ticket lifecycle including the thirteen statuses, the three actor kinds, the complete transition matrix and the service-level targets by priority.

Also covers, as context. The Demo Schedule approval chain in both on-site and virtual modes, BOM and procurement handoffs, project handover, and the ownership boundaries between teams.

Excludes. Technical Project Management itself, which is identified throughout as a connected module rather than part of Operations.

2.6 Partner Portal SOP

Covers. All seven partner areas in partner-friendly language: Dashboard, My Leads / Projects, Quotations, Demo Requests, Marketing Materials, Marketing Requests and Technical Support. Written for an external reader, with what happens on our side explained so partners know what to expect.

Distinctive content. Mandatory access and security guidance expressed as partner obligations; published support service targets; and clear statements of the four things partners cannot do, each with its reason.

Deliberately excludes. Internal security implementation, database structure, interface details, credentials and administrative configuration. Internal status vocabulary is never exposed — only the collapsed partner-facing positions.

05Reference submodules outside the six SOPs

The Feature Reference describes 52 modules. Twenty-four of them belong to departments for which no SOP was requested. They are listed here for completeness, with a note of where each is nonetheless touched as cross-module context.

Reference sectionSubmodulesTreatment in this SOP set

Billing is contrasted with the payment queue in Accounts §7.6. Quotations and Project Dashboard appear from the partner side. Partner Management is referenced in Administration §5.1. The remainder would need a Sales SOP.

Reports (5)Analytics, Target Details, Performance Review, Team Daily Reports, Partner AnalyticsTeam Daily Reports is referenced where it bounds HR's rights (HR §6.8.3, §4.4). Performance Review and Partner Analytics are located in Administration §5.1. The remainder would need a Reports SOP.
Recommendation

Three further SOPs would complete the platform: Sales, Technical Project Management and a combined Workspace & Reports guide. The six delivered documents already contain the cross-module material those would need to reference, so they can be written without revisiting this set.
